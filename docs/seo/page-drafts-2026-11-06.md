## 2. New page: call screening script generator

**Searches seen in autocomplete:** "call screening script", "iphone call screening script", "apple call screening script", "ios 26 call screening".

**Competition seen:** mostly consumer how-tos, plus a few vendor posts (Kixie, JustCall, ReadyMode, Dropcontact). I saw no complete script in the results. I still need to run the book's full check on each result before building.

**Address:** /tools/call-screening-script-generator

**Title (49):** Call Screening Script Generator: iPhone & Android

**H1:** Call screening script: what to say so they pick up.

**Answer under the headline (56 words):**
When an iPhone screens a call from an unknown number, it answers for the person and asks your name and reason for calling. They read your answer and decide whether to pick up. Say your real name, your company and one specific reason, in a single sentence. Pick your industry below to get three short versions.

**Tool:** the existing script generator's industries, with three spoken lines per industry. Spoken scripts stay in English because agents read them to US leads.

**Facts, from Apple's guide (read 6 Nov):**
- With Ask Reason for Calling on, calls from unknown numbers are asked for more information before the iPhone rings.
- After the caller shares their name and reason, the iPhone rings and shows the response.
- The person chooses Never, Ask Reason for Calling or Silence. Silence sends unsaved numbers to voicemail.

**Still to read before building:** Google's own page for Android call screening. The title mentions Android, so that word comes out if I can't confirm it.

**What the page won't claim:** any pickup rate. I found no measured figure, and I won't invent one.

**DialerSeat, once at the bottom:** "DialerSeat bills $35 a week per seat. A connected call reaches your agent's screen with the script for that campaign."

## 3. New page: cheapest power dialer

**Searches seen in autocomplete:** "cheapest power dialer", "low cost power dialer", "best cheap power dialer", "cheap dialer for cold calling".

**Competition seen:** vendor lists, several dated 2025. We hold the checked prices already.

**Address:** /faq/cheapest-power-dialer

**Title (50):** Cheapest Power Dialer: What You Actually Pay First

**H1:** Cheapest power dialer: it depends how long you dial.

**Answer under the headline (55 words):**
The cheapest power dialer depends on how long you need it. DialerSeat bills by the week, so a week costs $35. Dialers that bill by the month charge the whole month even if you dial for one week. Plans with annual terms charge a year up front. Compare the smallest amount you can actually spend.

**The table: smallest amount you can spend (each vendor's own page, checked 6 Nov 2026)**

| Dialer | Billed | Smallest purchase for one seat |
|---|---|---|
| DialerSeat | Weekly | $35, one week |
| BatchDialer | Monthly | $119, one month |
| WAVV Multi Line | Monthly | $149, one month |
| Mojo Triple Line + Agent Access | Monthly | $149, one month |
| PhoneBurner | Monthly | $165, one month |
| ReadyMode Starter | Monthly | $239, one month |

**The honest section, "When something else is cheaper":** if you dial every week for a long stretch, a low-priced monthly plan can cost less. Phone systems that add a single-line power dialer (CloudTalk, Aircall, Dialpad) can also cost less, but check which tier includes the dialer. The existing cost calculator shows the exact figures for your weeks and agents.

**What the page won't do:** state DialerSeat's price as a monthly figure, or quote a price I couldn't read on the vendor's own page.
