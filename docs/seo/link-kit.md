# Link kit: everything you need to start earning links

Built 6 Nov 2026 from the RankHonestly book (chapters 24 to 28). Nothing here has been sent or submitted. You send from your own accounts. The sites to pitch are in [link-targets.csv](link-targets.csv), which is also your tracking sheet.

**The routine:** 3 to 5 emails a day and 2 or 3 honest community answers a week. Expect about 2 links per 50 emails, showing up 8 to 12 weeks later. Send one follow-up after 5 to 7 days, then stop for good. If 100 well-targeted emails bring nothing, the sentence needs work, not more emails.

**Never:** pay for a link, offer a link swap, or post your link on a forum from a new account.

## Send order

1. **Compliance sites** (Template A). The state list page is the strongest thing you have to pitch.
2. **University sales programs** (Template B). The book says these are rarely pitched, and they link to free tools.
3. **Call centre sites** (Template C).
4. **Dialer comparison lists** (Template D). Hold these until the pricing index re-check is finished; see the end of this file. A writer who checks one wrong price stops reading.

## The one sentence

Use it word for word, everywhere:

Checked on ReadyMode's and Mojo's own pricing pages on 6 Nov 2026. DialerSeat's price is only ever stated in weeks.

### If they reply

- **"We don't add links to old posts."** Ask if anything upcoming fits, then leave it.
- **"How is this different from [a dialer]?"** Send the one sentence and how to check it. If it takes more than two sentences, it isn't working.
- **"Is this yours?"** Yes, say so immediately.
- **They ask for money.** Decline politely. That's buying a link.

## Community answers

Answer the question without linking. Say who you are when your product comes up, for example: "I run a dialer that bills by the week, so I'm biased, but what you're describing is..."

Threads worth reading first are marked `community` in the sheet. Check each forum's rules on self-promotion before you mention DialerSeat at all.
