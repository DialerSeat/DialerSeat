# What the competitors publish, and what none of them does (13 Nov 2026)

RankHonestly chapter 4: read the biggest competitors' sitemaps for what is missing. Their absences are decisions they made about what is worth building, published on their own sites.

Read on 13 Nov 2026 from each site's own sitemap: ReadyMode (320 addresses), Mojo (154), PhoneBurner (385), BatchDialer (521), Kixie (1,364), Convoso (796). CallTools publishes no sitemap. The URL lists were saved during the session and are not kept in the repo.

## What none of the six has

| Topic | What we found | What it means for DialerSeat |
|---|---|---|
| Calling rules in any other country | Nothing. Kixie has one page on international numbers; ReadyMode's only Canada page is a press release. | Explains why calling rules by country is our best cluster (about 30% of Google visitors on 11 Nov). Keep adding countries from primary sources. |
| Weekly billing, no contract, month to month | Nothing. ReadyMode has one post on flat-rate vs per-minute pricing. | Nobody contests the sentence. The contract-terms study (/data/dialer-contract-terms) is the evidence behind it. |
| Calculators and free tools | Convoso has an AI script generator and an ROI blog post; PhoneBurner one mortgage script generator. No calculators anywhere. | Our 17 tools have no direct competition from these six. |
| Offshore and remote agents, truck dispatch, virtual assistants | Nothing on offshore agents, truck dispatch or virtual assistants. PhoneBurner has two work-from-home posts. | Niche pages are uncontested here, but they still need search demand checked first. |
| "Calls going straight to voicemail", "calls not connecting" | Nothing by those names. | A failure-search gap; check the wording on forums before building. |

## What they do cover (so expect competition)

- **State laws.** ReadyMode has Florida and Maryland mini-TCPA posts and a fifty-states guide; Kixie has mini-TCPA laws by state and state Do Not Call lists; Convoso's state guide is behind an email form, which Google can't read. Our state pages compete with two blog posts and one gated PDF, not with a page per state.
- **Spam Likely, voicemail drop, abandoned calls.** Every one of them has these. Crowded.
- **CRM integrations.** Kixie has 185 integration pages (HubSpot, GoHighLevel and more), PhoneBurner 14. A page per CRM would be up against Kixie's whole section.
- **Area codes.** BatchDialer has 362 pages, one per area code ("local numbers"). That template is taken.
- **Verticals.** Real estate, insurance, mortgage, BPO, collections, political and nonprofit are all covered.

## Found on the way: people searching how to cancel a dialer

Google autocomplete (13 Nov) finishes "how to cancel …" for 13 of the dialers we compare: ReadyMode, Mojo, BatchDialer, Kixie, WAVV, JustCall, Aircall, Dialpad, RingCentral, GoHighLevel, Ringover, Nextiva and REDX, often as "… subscription" or "… contract". Not for PhoneBurner, Convoso, CloudTalk, Close, smrtPhone or Aloware.

None of the six sitemaps has a cancellation page. These are people leaving a dialer, which makes them the best possible prospects for one with no contract. Each page would have to state the vendor's own cancellation terms from its own help pages or terms of service, and nothing else. Not built yet: it needs Joshua's yes.
